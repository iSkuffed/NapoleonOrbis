Napoleon: Orbis - credits and licences

Map data
- Natural Earth (naturalearthdata.com): land, coastlines, lakes, rivers, minor islands, admin-1 provinces,
  populated places, glaciated areas, geography regions. Public domain.
- NOAA ETOPO 2022 Global Relief Model, 60 arc-second surface (ncei.noaa.gov): terrain height. Public domain (US Government).
- NASA Blue Marble Next Generation (July 2004 composite): ground colour.
  "The Blue Marble Next Generation data is courtesy of Reto Stockli (NASA/GSFC) and NASA's Earth Observatory." visibleearth.nasa.gov
- RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017 (ecoregions.appspot.com): ground and vegetation type. CC BY 4.0.
  Dinerstein, E., et al. 2017. An Ecoregion-Based Approach to Protecting Half the Terrestrial Realm. BioScience 67, 534-545.

Flags of the new nations: drawn by the mod's own tools (no external artwork).
