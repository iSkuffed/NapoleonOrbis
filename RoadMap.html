Napoleon: Orbis

A Napoleon: Total War mod that extends the campaign to the whole world, with the nations of c. 1805. Built independently from public knowledge and our own analysis of the shipped game files. Last updated 2026-10-18.

1. Where we stand

  • It has been done, so it is possible. Two separate 2026 projects show an enlarged map running in Napoleon: Distant Seas (one continuous global map, paywalled) and OpenETW (Empire's campaign map imported into Napoleon, on ModDB). Neither has published how. We build our own route and do not use paywalled material.
  • Engine plumbing survives. Napoleon's executable still contains Empire's whole theatre system (multiple map areas, transitions, trade theatres), all 33 Empire climates (American, Indian, Asian, arctic seas) with battle-terrain mappings, and Empire-format startpos files parse with our tools. Napoleon.exe is already Large-Address-Aware (4 GB).
  • Every map file in the game can now be read and written exactly by our own tools (tools/packtool.py, tools/esf.py): all 28 packs and all 34 campaign ESF files, byte for byte.
  • The real obstacle is generated data. Regions, pathfinding and AI files were produced by CA's in-house tools, which were never released. Pathfinding (pathfinding.esf, plus a copy inside startpos.esf) is where every past attempt to add regions stalled. Phase 3 exists to beat it.
  • Vanilla Grand Campaign: 72 land regions, 41 factions in the startpos (49 counting rebels), and a map world of 1280×640 units with a 750×385 playable theatre. Geographic scale is about 11.5 units per degree of longitude.

7. Phase 4 — World map bake pipeline

Goal: one reproducible command turns open geodata plus our curated region list into a complete campaign_maps/nap_world.

  1. Fetch and cache geodata (ETOPO 2022, Natural Earth 10m, ecoregions, Köppen, 1800 cities); record licences and attribution.
  2. Projection module (D2): lon/lat ↔ world units, plus the inverse for debugging. Unit-tested. done tools/proj.py; world land geometry tools/worldgeo.py (Natural Earth, 97% agreement with vanilla cells).
  3. Milestone M1 "world ocean": the whole world sailable, Europe unchanged, non-Europe land impassable (one filler land region), so the bake pipeline is proven in game before any new land region exists. Lanes: terrain (heightmap + supertexture, tools/bake_terrain.py), regions.esf + world sea zones (tools/regions_world.py), pathfinding (tools/pf_gen.py), sea grids, minimap rasters, startpos regrid. In game (2026-10-18): loads, 5 end-turns with AI turns, fleet orders route into the world ocean. Painted-mask terrain (no parchment frame) done. The 38 named world seas are in the startpos (tools/world_zones.py, stages abrndeg): loads, 3 AI turns. Parked: the 6 CAI boundaries between vanilla and new seas (crash at 0xD6D9E0, or a hang with full per-region beliefs), so the AI does not plan across that seam. done
  4. Land regions continent by continent after M1 (Phase 5 adds them to the startpos). Gate G4 (region count) runs then, since Lean 228 sits under the ~250 palette ceiling.
  5. Region budget (agreed 2026-10-19): EU4/EU5-style density. Sparse areas stay a few large regions: Siberia 4, Canada 5, Kongo/central Africa 5, Amazon 1, Tibet/inner Asia 3, Australia 2, Sahara 1 + corridors (Lean profile). Lean everywhere is about 226 of the 252 lookup-palette colours, so about 26 are spare for denser Europe (German and Italian states, Balkans, Poland). Densely political areas (India, East Indies, Caribbean) are the real trade-off against Europe. The Americas were built Full (52); --compact returns about 12. G4 result: there is no 256 region cap, only the 252-colour minimap palette. Small extra regions (e.g. denser Europe) take a minimap_parent colour.
  6. Deserts and other barriers (agreed 2026-10-19): the Sahara is impassable except for caravan corridors (data/world1805/routes.json: Adrar road, Tafilalt–Timbuktu, Tripoli–Ghadames–Kano, Tripoli–Fezzan–Bornu, the Forty Days Road; the Nile is passable). Corridors are passable strips inside existing regions, so they cost no regions. Built with the Africa wave; they must be several pathfinding cells wide and pass pf_check.py. The same mechanism covers Himalayan passes, the Gobi, the Andes and the Australian interior. Desert attrition is optional polish, once we know what triggers it.
  7. Heightmap and land/sea mask, tuned to match vanilla's height style (vanilla range 0–226, exaggerated relief).
  8. Region polygons: a curated GeoJSON (data/regions_1805.geojson) with keys, owners, capitals, ports and climate overrides. Lookup colours are assigned automatically.
  9. Emit regions.esf, pathfinding.esf, sea_grids, poi, trade_routes and the metadata grid with the Phase 3 generators.
  10. Display layers: borders, roads, rivers, coastline mesh, supertexture (terrain painting by climate and ground type), fog, snow and trees.
  11. UI rasters: campaign map, strategic radar and lookup images.
  12. Visual review: render the baked map next to vanilla Europe at the same zoom. Europe must still look like Napoleon.

9. Phase 6 — Nations content

Major new powers are the USA, Qing, Mughal and Maratha states, Persia, Tokugawa Japan, Morocco, Sokoto, Kingdom of Hawaii and others (full list in research/04). Work: unit rosters reusing existing Empire-era unit models where they exist in Napoleon's packs, flags, faction colours, portraits, AI-only versus playable flags, diplomacy defaults, and rebels per culture. Napoleon's UI allows only 4 playable factions per startpos, so the playable set is chosen deliberately.

Future idea (user, 2026-10-20): asset importer, Tale of Two Wastelands style. An optional installer step that finds another Total War game the player owns (Empire first: its Warscape-era units, such as Indian, Native American and Ottoman ones, are closest to Napoleon's formats) and converts unit models and textures on the player's machine for the new nations. Nothing from CA is redistributed, which is the same rule the loc merge already follows. Needs a format-compatibility probe first. Later-engine titles (Shogun 2 and on) are likely much harder.

Faction select (research/11): the 4-faction cap is the frontend layout (4 flag slots in faction_selection.luac), not the startpos or the exe. A replacement Lua using Empire's leftover 9-flag carousel, shipped as plain text under the .luac name, loads in game. All 8 test factions are selectable, and a campaign as Denmark runs 2 turns. Next: world startpos with new nations playable, plus victory-condition entries for them. probe passed

  • Open-alpha blockers (user, 2026-10-20): the Aral Sea, Lake Balkhash and Lake Baikal are walkable land (make them impassable or water); Karelia (asi_olonets) has no border with Finland. Fixed 2026-10-30: the weld now borders Finland.

A new faction russian_american_company holds ame_russian_america/Sitka as Russia's protectorate, and east_india_company is a British protectorate. Both are set in the startpos (tools/protectorates.py, a copy of egy_napoleon's Ottoman/Mameluke encoding). In game: 8 AI turns ran as Russia (2026-10-28). Whether Russia still sends fleets to Alaska has not been watched yet. done
  • Other researched candidates (2026-10-21), not yet approved: Pacific sea lanes across the seam (research/14, Empire's theatre transitions are still live in the exe); world trees (research/15, a 41k-tree test file is installed in the playtest pack); fog radius, extra farm/resource slots and the reservoir filter (research/16, in progress).
  • Automated soak test: end-turn loops run headless; track turn time, memory use (32-bit, 4 GB ceiling) and crashes.
  • AI behaviour across oceans: invasion targets, trade, naval movement. Distant Seas reports that AI awareness of new regions needed binary-level work, so expect the same.
  • Balance pass on economy, attrition and movement over the larger map.

11. Phase 8 — Packaging and release

  • Free and open, no paywall. Public release, with Windows and Linux (Proton) as equal targets.
  • One installer for both platforms, written in Go and cross-compiled from Linux to a Windows .exe and a Linux binary, with no runtime needed. It finds the Steam library (also the Proton prefix for user.script.txt), installs the mod pack, writes the UTF-16LE user.script.txt line, places the loose startpos, merges our names into the player's own localisation.loc (a type-2 pack, so no CA text is shipped) and applies the exe patch. Uninstall reverses every step.
  • Exe patch (tools/exe_patch.py, approved 2026-10-19): widens the building and tree display boxes from x,z ±1000 to ±2048. Groups added since: terrain, pfgrid, zoom and astar; the installer applies them all. It patches only the known Steam exe (sha256 check), keeps a backup, and has to be re-run after a Steam "verify files".
  • Release build: installer/release.sh WORLD VERSION makes the payload, runs the installer tests and writes dist/NapoleonOrbis-VERSION-{windows,linux}.zip (binary with the payload embedded, README_PLAYERS.txt, CREDITS.txt). 0.9.0 = pt_w14. Tested 2026-11-01: the Windows binary under Wine installed into the real game (exe byte-identical to exe_patch.py, startpos backed up), and a new Coalition campaign started from it. 12. Risks

Pathfinding cannot be generated. Mitigation: decompile the loader and consumer in Napoleon.exe; check whether the engine rebuilds missing pathfinding data at load. Last resort: hand-author only the coarse navigation layers the engine actually reads.
Hard-coded size or region limits. Mitigation: the Phase 2 probes run before generator work; fall back to separate theatres (G5) or a smaller scale. Found: building and tree display boxes of ±1000 (settlements beyond them crash). Handled by the exe patch (Phase 8).
The AI ignores new regions or cannot cross oceans. Mitigation: keep CAI data in sync with the Phase 5 validator; binary analysis with REA if data alone is not enough.
Supertexture and coastline art cost. Mitigation: generate procedurally from climate and ground type; vanilla Europe stays untouched where possible.
Automated testing under Proton. Mitigation: headless gamescope harness (task 1.1); fall back to scripted runs on the visible desktop.
