# 17. pt_w10: world fog maps, no reservoirs, world trees

pt_w10 is pt_w9t (pt_w9 plus 41k trees) with three display-side fixes. Regions, pathfinding, the db tables and loc are
byte-identical to pt_w9 (`diff -rq` against work/mods/pt_w9t lists only the 5 display files below).

## 1. Fog sight maps (research/16 Q1): tools/region_los.py

This is a post-pass on a built startpos:

    python tools/region_los.py IN.esf REGIONS.esf OUT.esf

Vanilla's rule, measured on the input itself, is that a REGION cell is lit iff its corner point lies in the region
polygon grown by 10 u, with the box snapped outward. The SETTLEMENT disc is one fixed 69-cell 9x9 pattern.

- Self-check on pt_w9: the 45 lit vanilla regions give a cell IoU of min 0.999 (eur_bretagne) and mean 1.000. Every
  lit vanilla settlement disc equals the pattern.
- Redrawn:
  - 221 region maps: the 194 new ones, plus the 27 dark vanilla ones switched on.
  - 233 settlement discs, 39 of them switched on.
- Every one of the 266 regions now starts lit, so factions made playable later are covered. Lit vanilla maps are untouched.
- Run cost: 20 s, 0.98 GB.

## 2. Reservoirs (research/16 Q3)

- worldgeo.NATURAL_LAKES holds 28 cited NE names: Il'men', Mjøsa, Zaysan, Oulujärvi, Imandra, Lac Saint-Jean,
  Arrow Lakes, the NZ hydro lakes, and others.
- The other 202 'Reservoir' features become land, adding 2500.7 u² of land; land goes from 6941 to 6895 polygons.
- This applies only to `worldgeo.land(natural=True)`, cached as land_display.wkb. It is read by bake_terrain and gen_trees.
- `worldgeo.land()` still removes every lake, so land.wkb is byte-identical. The first attempt replaced land.wkb, and
  the byte-level gate failed: same geometry (XOR area 0), but different WKB vertex order.
- bake_terrain.dams() also marks the dropped reservoirs as water in bmng_prep. Without it, BMNG's dark reservoir water
  leaves black scars. See shots/reservoirs_preview_before_after.png.
- Gate (work/scratch/features/res_verify.py), all PASS:
  - land.wkb equals the backup;
  - land() rebuilt from NE equals land.wkb;
  - the lake-filled outline is identical.
- Rebake: heightmap 97 s / 3.4 GB, supertexture 489 s / 10.9 GB peak (the bmng_prep rebuild; 3.7 GB with the cache),
  detail 4 s. `bake_terrain check` OK.
- Residue: a thin dark line remains along the lower Volga, from BMNG river pixels outside the NE polygon.

## 3. Trees: tools/gen_trees.py, wired into world_build.py

- `world_build.py --trees-for pt_w10 [--display-only]` copies the rasters and writes display/trees/campaign.rigid_trees.
  A full build goes to nap_world.new and is swapped in at the end.
- Placement rules:
  - trees grow on land outside vanilla's painted sheet;
  - the 8,903 vanilla trees are kept;
  - density is s0.35;
  - no new tree within 2.3 u of any settlement, town, port or dock, vanilla ones included (1819 points from the variant's
    startpos and build_info; the vanilla towns took 6 more generated trees off, mainly at Belgorod, which lies outside the sheet).
- Result: 43,149 instances (34,246 new), 0.72 MB, 3 s with the biome cache.
